Getting Started

Welcome to Smart Revision Scheduler – your personalised study timetable tailored to your needs.

To begin, click Start New Timetable on the home page. If you have already created a timetable, select Load Timetable.

Follow these three steps:

1. Add your subjects, exam dates and confidence levels

2. Enter your daily availability for studying

3. Add any commitments that block out study time

Adding Subjects

Each subject requires a name, exam date and confidence level.

You may add optional notes for future reference, for example: 'Focus on Paper 2 Topics' or 'Review practicals'.

Notes will appear when you hover over a subject block in your timetable, helping you remember what to prioritise.

Use Add Subject to include multiple subjects. You can edit or delete subjects at any time from the Summary & Review page.

Choosing Your Confidence Level

Confidence levels help the system prioritise which subjects need more study time. Use the table below as a guide:

Confidence Level Description Typical Recent Score
High You have strong understanding and rarely struggle with the content. A*/ A (70%+)
Medium You understand most topics but still have some gaps or inconsistencies. B / C (50–69%)
Low You find the subject challenging or scored poorly in recent tests. D or below (under 50%)

How it affects your timetable: Subjects with Low confidence receive more revision sessions throughout the week, while High confidence subjects get fewer sessions to maintain your knowledge.

Setting Availability

Availability defines when you are free to study each day. For each day you wish to study, set one start time and one end time.

Important: You can only set one availability slot per day. If you have a break in the middle of your day (e.g., lunch, dinner, or other activities), add those breaks as commitments instead.

For example:

  • Availability: Monday 09:00 – 22:00
  • Commitments: Lunch (12:00 – 13:00), Dinner (18:00 – 19:00)

The scheduler will only generate study sessions within your availability times and will automatically avoid all commitments.

You can select multiple days at once when adding availability (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday can share the same time slot).

Adding Commitments

Commitments block out times when you cannot study. Use commitments for anything that takes up your time, such as:

  • Work or part-time jobs
  • Sports practice or gym sessions
  • Meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
  • Social events or appointments
  • Sleep or personal time

Each commitment requires:

  • Name – What the commitment is (e.g., "Football Practice", "Dinner")
  • Date – When it occurs
  • Start/End Time – The duration of the commitment
  • Repeat Pattern – How often it repeats

Repeat Patterns:

  • One-Time – The commitment only occurs on the specified date
  • Weekly – Repeats every week on the same day (e.g., every Monday)
  • Daily – Repeats every single day until your last exam

Tip: Add recurring commitments like meals as Daily or Weekly to avoid manually entering them for each day. The system will automatically exclude these times from your study schedule.

Summary & Review

After entering all your subjects, availability and commitments, click Save and Continue to view the Summary & Review page.

This page displays all your inputs in organised tables. You can:

  • Edit any record by clicking the Edit button
  • Delete any record by clicking the ❌ icon
  • Add more subjects, availability or commitments if needed

Once you're satisfied with your inputs, click Generate Timetable to create your personalised study plan.

Viewing Your Timetable

Your generated timetable appears as a weekly calendar grid showing all your revision sessions. Each session displays:

  • Subject name
  • Start and end time
  • Colour coding (each subject has a unique colour)

Use the arrow buttons to navigate between weeks. On mobile, you can swipe through individual days for a clearer view.

Hover over any session to see your subject notes (if you added any).

Regenerating Your Timetable

If your exam dates, availability or commitments change, you can regenerate your timetable at any time.

Go to Summary & Review, make your changes, then click Generate Timetable again. The system will create a fresh schedule based on your updated information.

Alternatively, click Regenerate Timetable from the Timetable Dashboard to instantly create a new version with the same inputs.

Exporting Your Timetable

You can export your timetable as a CSV file for use in other applications like Excel or Google Sheets.

Click Export Timetable on the Timetable Dashboard. The exported file includes:

  • Date of each session
  • Day of the week
  • Subject name
  • Start and end times

This allows you to print your schedule, share it with others, or import it into calendar apps.

How It Works

Step 1: Input Collection

The system collects three main types of information:

  • Subjects – Your exam subjects, dates, and confidence levels
  • Availability – One time slot per day when you can study (e.g., 09:00 – 22:00)
  • Commitments – Specific times when you are busy and cannot study

All data is stored in a local database and persists between sessions.

Step 2: Scheduling Algorithm

When you click Generate Timetable, the system uses a priority-based scheduling algorithm that:

  • Prioritises subjects with low confidence – Subjects you struggle with receive more revision sessions
  • Considers exam proximity – Subjects with sooner exams are scheduled more frequently as the date approaches
  • Spaces out sessions – The algorithm avoids clustering all sessions for one subject on a single day
  • Respects availability – Sessions are only placed within your specified study times
  • Avoids commitments – The system never schedules sessions that overlap with your commitments
  • Stops after exams – Once an exam date passes, that subject is removed from future scheduling

Step 3: Time Slot Allocation

The algorithm works through your availability day by day:

  • It identifies free time blocks within your availability
  • It subtracts any commitments from those blocks
  • It assigns revision sessions to the remaining free slots
  • Sessions are typically 1-2 hours long depending on available space
  • The system balances subjects across the week to prevent overloading any single day

Step 4: Output & Display

The generated timetable is displayed as a visual weekly grid on the Timetable Dashboard:

  • Each subject is assigned a unique colour for easy identification
  • Sessions appear as coloured blocks at their scheduled times
  • A legend below the timetable shows which colour represents which subject
  • You can navigate between weeks to see your full revision schedule

Step 5: Flexibility & Regeneration

Your timetable is not fixed. You can:

  • Regenerate it at any time to create a different schedule
  • Edit your inputs (subjects, availability, commitments) and regenerate
  • Export it as CSV to modify manually or import into other tools

The system saves all your inputs automatically, so you can return to adjust your schedule whenever needed.

Key Design Principles

  • One availability slot per day – Simplifies scheduling and prevents conflicts
  • Commitments handle breaks – Use commitments to block out meals, breaks, or other activities within your availability window
  • Confidence-based weighting – Ensures you spend more time on subjects you find challenging
  • Exam date awareness – Allocates more sessions to subjects with approaching exams
  • No overlaps – The system guarantees no scheduling conflicts between study sessions and commitments
