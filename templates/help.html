Getting Started

Welcome to Smart Revision Scheduler – your personalised study timetable personalised to you.

To begin, click Start New Timetable on the home page. If you have already created a timetable select Load Timetable

1. Add your subjects, exam dates and confidence level

2. Enter your weekly availability to study

3. Add any commitments like work, dinner or sports

Adding Subjects

Each subject requires a name, exam date and confidence level.

You may add notes for future reference, for example

'Focus on Paper 2 Topics'

Notes will appear when you hover over a subject in your timetable, helping you remember what to focus on.

Use Add Subject to include mutliple subjects

Choosing Your Confidence Level

Use the table below to help you decide your confidence level for each subject:

Confidence Level Description Typical Recent Score
High You have strong understanding and rarely struggle with the content. A*/ A (70%+)
Medium You understand most topics but still have some gaps or inconsistencies. B / C (50–69%)
Low You find the subject challenging or scored poorly in recent tests. D or below (under 50%)

Setting Availability

Availability is the time of day when you can study. Choose your start and end time for each day you wish to study.

The scheduler will only generate sessions inside your availability and avoid overlapping instances.

Adding Commitments

Commitments block out time you cannot study. Each commitment includes:

  • Name of commitment
  • Date
  • Start/End Time
  • Nature of repetition (Daily, Weekly, Custom Days)

If you pick Daily, it repeats every day until exam finish. If you pick Weekly, it repeats the same day every week. If you select Custom, you can choose specific days manually.

Summary

After entering all details, select Save and Continue to see a summary page with all your inputs. You can press edit, if you wish to change any details When ready, click Generate Timetable to create your personalised study plan.

Saving & Exporting

Your timetable is saved automatically in your browser’s local storage.

You can reload it anytime using Load Existing Timetable from the home screen.

To print or share it, use Export Timetable to save as PDF or CSV.

How It Works

Step 1: Input Collection

The system takes in three main types of information:

  • Subjects – Your exam subjects and when the exams are
  • Availability – The times you are free to study
  • Commitments – Fixed times when you cannot study

Step 2: Processing

When you click Generate Timetable, the system uses a scheduling algorithm:

  • It gives greater weighting to subjects with lower confidence levels.
  • It considers exam dates so subjects with sooner exams receive higher priority.
  • Study sessions are spaced to avoid overcrowding.
  • It prevents study periods for subjects once the exam date has passed.

Step 3: Output

The generated timetable appears on the dashboard as a weekly calendar view. Each session is colour-coded by subject and shows:

  • Subject name
  • Session duration
  • Confidence weighting

Step 4: Saving & Regeneration

All entered subjects, commitments and preferences are stored in your browser.

You can regenerate your timetable if exam dates or availability change, using the Regenerate Timetable button.

You can choose to export the timetable from the dashboard as a PDF or CSV

Algorithm Explanation

The system uses a priority-based greedy approach:

  • Subjects sorted by exam proximity and confidence
  • Loops through avaialble slots
  • Sessions allocated into free availability blocks ensuring no overlap
  • Repeats until all slots are filled or until subjects are balanced throughout the week
